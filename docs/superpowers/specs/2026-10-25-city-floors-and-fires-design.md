# Ruined City: 5-floor cap and street fires - design

## Reuse check
three.js has a volumetric `Fire` example (needs a noise texture asset); chosen instead: small procedural noise shaders (no asset, no dependency), plus a plain `PointLight` for the dynamic glow.

## Requirements
1. **Floors.** A building's floors are its `windowRows`. Every building has 3-5 rows. Height is derived, not hand-set: `height = 4 + windowRows * 3.3` (about 14-21 m). Specs keep four buildings per side, varied heights, all roof styles.
2. **Fires.** `src/scene/cityFire.ts`: `getCityFireSpots()` (three deterministic fires: two on the existing wrecks, one burning debris pile beside the road, all at least 6 m from the hero at the origin and inside the street) and `fireFlicker(time, seed, amount)` (multiplier around 1, bounded, deterministic, `amount = 0` gives exactly 1).
3. **`src/scene/Fire.tsx`.** Per fire: crossed flame planes with an additive procedural fire shader (scrolling noise, hot core, red edge), a few animated embers (points shader), a dark smoke column (alpha-noise shader), and an orange `PointLight` whose intensity follows `fireFlicker`. No hooks that need a canvas: time and light are driven from the flame mesh `onBeforeRender` so the existing element-tree tests still work.
4. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: flicker amount 0 and shader time frozen.

## Failure behaviour
If a fire shader fails to compile, three logs it and the plane is skipped; the point light still lights the street.

## Acceptance criteria
- No building has more than 5 window rows; heights match the formula.
- Three animated fires visible; nearby surfaces and the robot visibly brighten and dim with the flicker.
- Other stages unchanged; no console errors; tsc, vitest, build, Playwright pass.

## Tests
Unit: floor cap and height formula; fire spots (count, clearance, bounds, determinism); flicker (bounds, determinism, variation, amount 0). Existing stage/backdrop tests updated for the new height range.

## Amendment: performance (user request)
Rendering was slow, so only one fire stays live (front right, the debris fire at the right kerb: flame shader, embers, smoke, flickering point light). The two wreck fires become static glow spots: a baked `public/textures/ember-glow.png` (generated by `npm run glow:generate`) on an additive flat disc plus two low crossed planes on top of each wreck. No lights, no shaders, no per-frame work. `CityFireSpot` gains `kind: 'live' | 'glow'`; exactly one live spot. Flames are also small and nearly burnt out (low, patchy, red, dim light) as the damage is long past.

Tests: exactly one live spot and two glow spots; the glow PNG ships with the expected size.
